Slide size:
- 1280x720 landscape.

Background system:
- Warm off-white canvas (#F7F4EE) with deep ink header text (#172033).
- Dark cover/section panels use #0D2232 and #123B4E with teal/cyan accents.

Typography:
- Chinese-first sans: PingFang SC for titles, body, labels.
- Title range: 34-54 px; cover: 58-66 px; body: 17-24 px; labels: 12-15 px.

Palette:
- Ink: #172033
- Deep teal: #0D3B4A
- Signal cyan: #11A6B3
- Safety amber: #F2A33A
- Alarm red: #D9493F
- Paper: #F7F4EE
- Muted gray: #667085
- Hairline: #D7D3C8

Chart and diagram grammar:
- Product/data flow diagrams use concrete lanes: 前端控制台, BT 后端, SY 后端, Redis Streams, Windows Agent, 现场设备.
- Connectors encode data/control movement only. Cyan means status/data path; amber/red means alert/control risk boundary.
- Cards are used for repeated recommendation items only; page sections remain open layouts.

Image grammar:
- UI screenshots are cropped inside clean device-like frames with thin hairline borders.
- Screenshots are paired with short callouts, not long captions.

Footer grammar:
- Bottom-left source note in 10 px muted gray; bottom-right page marker.

Banned motifs:
- No fake logo, no decorative gradient blobs, no stock imagery, no invented performance metric.
